ALL-SEASONS CARD UPDATE
=======================

Included
--------
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js
- questions.js
- assets/cards/spring/ (50 supplied Spring food cards)
- assets/cards/summer/ (42 unique supplied Summer food cards)
- assets/cards/autumn/ (55 supplied Autumn food cards)
- assets/cards/winter/ (43 supplied Winter food cards)

What changed
------------
- The game now contains 12 stages: 4 seasons multiplied by Easy, Medium and Hard.
- Selecting a season opens a difficulty-selection popup before the adventure begins.
- Easy uses familiar foods, Medium introduces less familiar foods, and Hard focuses on uncommon foods.
- Correct and incorrect answers are drawn from the selected difficulty tier.
- The title screen now uses a bright, child-friendly pop design with a clean sky-blue background, one simple sun shape and vivid seasonal cards.
- Selected seasons receive a colour-coded frame and the start button names the chosen season.
- The four tractors retain their character names: Robbie, Brian, Yanko and Graham.
- The game field now uses vivid green grass and a clearly separated warm-brown gravel road.
- Quiz panels, progress markers and controls use the same Nordic storybook palette.
- Spring now chooses 5 questions at random from all 50 supplied cards.
- Summer now chooses 5 questions at random from all 42 unique supplied cards.
- Autumn now chooses 5 questions at random from all 55 supplied cards.
- Winter now chooses 5 questions at random from all 43 supplied cards.
- A food will not repeat within one game.
- Closely related duplicates (for example Papaw/Papaya) are kept apart in one run.
- Questions now ask which food belongs to the selected season, using five season-specific phrasings.
- Wrong answers are drawn from other seasons and exclude foods that also appear in the selected season.
- A light growing-place clue remains visible below every question.
- YUMMIE reveals a stronger hint on request, combining the food's first letter with its detailed clue.
- The grass and gravel road use strong, high-visibility colours and distinct textures so younger children can recognise them immediately.
- A correct answer opens the matching card.
- The card shows Origin and Grows information.
- NEXT replaces the old 1.5-second automatic transition, so the player has time to read.
- The question and clue appear on separate lines.
- YUMMIE gives a different origin-based hint instead of repeating the clue.
- The tractor remains fixed at the bottom while field objects move down the screen.
- The quiz panel stays hidden until the first real question is ready; placeholder text is removed.

How to install
--------------
Copy these four files and the assets/cards/spring, assets/cards/summer, assets/cards/autumn and assets/cards/winter folders into the existing game
project. Keep the existing assets/tractors, assets/scenery and assets/yummie.png
files, because those original artwork files were not part of this upload.

Summer upload note
------------------
The Summer uploads contained 50 files, but 8 were byte-for-byte duplicate copies
of earlier cards. The game includes the 42 unique card files. Two different cards
are both labelled Passion Fruit; both are retained, but their shared group prevents
them from appearing together in one five-question game.

Notes on Origin
---------------
The country is a short, child-friendly reference to the commonly accepted place
of origin, domestication, early cultivation or (where a broad food category makes
that impossible) its best-known early cultivation. Ancient crops do not always
have one universally agreed modern country of origin.
